Felt · how the poker bot works

First it studies, then it plays

There are two parts. Before any real game, Felt studies millions of pretend hands until it can guess how good any poker spot is. While it plays, it stops and works out the best move for the exact spot it's in — and when looking too far ahead gets too hard, it just uses its good guess instead.

Why it works on a normal computer

Because every spot keeps all the hands together in one bundle, a round of practice is just one big pile of math done all at once — not one hand at a time. That kind of math runs on almost any computer: a fast graphics card if you have one, or a plain setting if you don't. The run in the picture took 95.9 seconds on a MacBook Air.
